# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Bucharest-area apartment owners renting long-term, who want one good tenant who signs, stays for years, and pays reliably. They're comparing Rent UP against "doing nothing" or DIY approaches (ANAF contracts, etc.), and want proof — ROI numbers, portfolio results, a concrete process — before booking an audit.

## Product Purpose

Rent UP optimizes apartments for long-term rental: interior design tailored to attract and retain quality tenants, plus a complete management system (4-pillar tenant selection, customized contracts, timestamped photo PV/inventory, dispute protocols). Success = an owner books an audit/package and lands a stable tenant who stays for years with predictable income.

## Brand Personality

Confident, direct, no-nonsense. Sells through systems, protocols, and ROI rather than vague reassurance. Tone is practical and evidence-based, grounded in the Romanian rental market, not salesy. Visually: refined editorial restraint (light-weight display type, warm cream + terracotta palette) paired with a direct, protocol-driven voice — the calm comes from competence, not soft language.

## Anti-references

Avoid cold corporate/SaaS dashboard aesthetics — no enterprise blue, gradient hero panels, dense data-table chrome, or generic tech-startup visual language. Also avoid generic real-estate-agency tropes (stock photography, listing-grid layouts, salesy banners).

## Design Principles

- Show the system, not just promises — process, protocols, and concrete before/after results (portfolio, ROI numbers) carry more weight than adjectives.
- Numbers and proof over claims — ROI percentages, timeframes (72h audit), concrete stats earn trust with skeptical owners.
- Calm confidence through restraint — light typography weights, warm neutral palette, generous spacing signal competence without shouting.
- Practical Romanian-market grounding — copy and case studies are concrete and locally specific (ANAF, PV foto, etc.), not generic SaaS phrasing.
- Warm but not soft — directness in copy and structure, warmth in palette and texture, never combine the two into vague reassurance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target: sufficient color contrast (especially body text against the cream backgrounds), keyboard-navigable interactive elements (calculator, nav), respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for animations/transitions, Romanian-language content with proper `lang` attributes.
