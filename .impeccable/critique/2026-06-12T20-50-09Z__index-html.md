## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Mostly not AI slop. The page executes a deliberate, documented system ("The Warm Protocol") faithfully in palette, typography, motion (asymmetric Lift Reflex hover timings), and the Ledger Color Rule. The tells that ARE present are inconsistencies with the site's OWN documented system rather than generic AI-template signals: (1) two back-to-back numbered "01/02/03..." patterns (Process steps + "De ce funcționează" reasons) competing for the same visual device for sequential vs. non-sequential content; (2) the uppercase-tracked eyebrow tag (`.sec-tag`) repeated on ~12 of 14 sections, contradicting the "rarity = signal" logic the system applies to its own accent color; (3) dead CSS for a fully-styled, unused `.audit-block`/`.ab-*` component (lines ~292-302, 368, 447, 662-663), evidence of an unreconciled redesign; (4) 44 em dashes throughout copy (testimonial/case-study headers, section dividers), directly contradicting the project's stated "no em dashes" copy rule, and a classic LLM-output tell.

**Deterministic scan**: `node .claude/skills/impeccable/scripts/detect.mjs --json index.html` failed both times with `Error: bundled detector not found` (exit 1) — the bundled detector entrypoint is missing from this installation. Per the critique flow, this is reported as "deterministic scan unavailable" and does not fail the run; manual review covered the equivalent ground above.

**Visual overlays**: No browser-automation tooling (Playwright/Chrome DevTools MCP, etc.) is available in this session, so the live-server + injected-overlay step could not run. No user-visible browser overlay was created; all findings above come from source review of `index.html` (1780 lines) and `DESIGN.md`.

## Overall Impression

The page is a faithful, well-crafted execution of a genuinely good system: the case studies are the standout, doing exactly what PRODUCT.md asks ("show the system, not just promises") with line-item budgets and real timelines. The biggest gap is structural pacing, not visual polish: two consecutive multi-input calculators land before any trust-building proof, asking a skeptical visitor to "do work" before the site has earned it. The second-biggest gap is a handful of unreconciled leftovers (em dashes, dead CSS, doubled numbering, an over-applied eyebrow) that are individually small but collectively suggest the page hasn't had a final consistency pass against its own DESIGN.md.

## What's Working

1. **Case studies (`.cs` blocks)** — situație-inițială → ce-am-schimbat → results → quote, with real budget breakdowns (e.g., "Serviciu design 3.300€ + Renovare 5.000€ + Mobilier 4.100€ = 12.400€") and concrete timelines (4 zile to tenant, 48 luni payback). This is the brand's "numbers and proof over claims" principle executed perfectly.
2. **The `mkt-note` calculator guardrail** — when a user's rent is already at the market ceiling, `calcOptRent()` returns an honest warning instead of an inflated number, reinforcing "evidence-based, not salesy."
3. **Asymmetric hover timing (Lift Reflex Rule)** — entrance (~200ms) vs exit (~400-420ms) transitions are consistently coded across `.pkg`, `.ba`, `.tc` — a subtle motion detail matching DESIGN.md's "intră rapid, ies lent" rule precisely.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Two stacked, multi-input calculators sit immediately after the hero, before any trust-building content.**
  **Why it matters**: `#cost-inactiune` and `#calc` together ask for 7 interactive inputs (rent, m², city, property type, time horizon, etc.) before `#fit` (Client Fit), `#process`, or any testimonial/case study appears. A skeptical landlord hasn't yet been told the company is credible, yet is asked to "do work" twice — this is the main driver of the cognitive-load checklist's 4 failures (Single focus, One thing at a time, Minimal choices, Working memory).
  **Fix**: Move `#fit` and a condensed proof element (one case-study stat or testimonial) ahead of the calculators, or merge the two calculators into one tabbed component ("Cost of staying put" vs "Gain from optimizing" as two views of the same inputs).
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout index.html` (or `/impeccable distill` if the goal is to cut to one calculator)

- **[P1] 44 em dashes throughout copy contradict the project's own "no em dashes" copy rule.**
  **Why it matters**: Section dividers, testimonial headers, and case-study titles (e.g., "2 camere Drumul Taberei — de la apartament distrus la chiriaș în 4 zile") all use em dashes — a known LLM-output tell that undercuts the "direct, no-nonsense, not salesy" voice the brand is built on.
  **Fix**: Replace em dashes with periods, colons, or restructured sentences (e.g., "2 camere Drumul Taberei: chiriaș găsit în 4 zile, după un apartament distrus.").
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify index.html`

- **[P1] Dead CSS for a fully-styled `.audit-block`/`.ab-*` component (lines ~292-302, 368, 447, 662-663) is unused in the markup.**
  **Why it matters**: The Audit offer is instead a card inside `.pkg-g`, competing visually with Standard/Premium even though it's a different kind of commitment (a low-cost diagnostic vs. paid design packages). The unused CSS suggests this was redesigned without reconciling the layout decision, and the current "Audit as a third package card" may undersell the audit's role as a low-commitment entry point.
  **Fix**: Either remove the dead CSS, or restore a standalone Audit CTA block visually distinct from the two paid packages.
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout index.html`

- **[P2] Eyebrow tag (`.sec-tag`) used on ~12 of 14 sections, contradicting the system's own "rarity = signal" logic.**
  **Why it matters**: The repeated pulsing-dot + uppercase Roasted-Clay label becomes wallpaper, training the eye to skip it exactly where it could help distinguish section types (calculator vs. testimonial vs. FAQ).
  **Fix**: Reserve the eyebrow for sections that need contextual framing (calculators, FAQ, Packages) and drop it from self-explanatory sections (Portfolio, Testimonials, About, Process) — cutting to 5-6 uses.
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable quieter index.html`

- **[P2] Double "01/02/03..." numbering pattern used back-to-back for sequential (Process) and non-sequential (Reasons) content.**
  **Why it matters**: `#process` (4 sequential steps) is immediately followed by "De ce funcționează" (5 independent value props), both numbered 01+. Numbering the Reasons implies an order/priority that doesn't exist and visually dilutes the Process section's legitimate use of numbering.
  **Fix**: De-number the Reasons section (use the `.rn` slot for an icon or short label instead), reserving sequential numerals for Process.
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset index.html`

## Persona Red Flags

**Skeptical Bucharest owner (project-specific)**: First real interaction after the hero is `#cost-inactiune` then `#calc` — being asked for rent, m², city, and property type twice before reading a single proof point. The strongest "we're not salespeople" trust signal (`.fit-note`, "Nu colaborăm pentru a face un apartament DOAR mai frumos...") is buried as an italic footnote under two bullet lists in `#fit`. At Packages, there's no link back to the ROI/cost-of-inaction numbers computed earlier, forcing mental math to judge "is 30€/mp worth it for my place."

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: `.hero-r{display:none}` at ≤900px removes the hero's before/after visual and "+200€" badge entirely on mobile, losing the strongest opening proof point. The `#btt` back-to-top only appears after 400px scroll and only returns to the top, no "jump to packages/contact" shortcut on a 16-section page. The 3-button "Oraș" group wraps awkwardly at ≤500px (`flex: none; width: calc(50% - 4px)`), creating a 2-up-plus-orphan layout.

**Riley (Deliberate Stress Tester)**: Setting calculator inputs to trigger the `mkt-note` ceiling warning still computes and displays a near-zero "Câștig suplimentar estimat" without explaining why the gain dropped, beyond the generic ceiling message. The dead `.audit-block` CSS is exactly the kind of unfinished-iteration evidence a code-level reviewer would flag as undercutting the "procedural rigor" brand promise.
